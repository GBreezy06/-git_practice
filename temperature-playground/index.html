2 Build your request

1.0
0 · predictable1 · default2 · random

Low (near 0): more deterministic. The model sticks to its most likely words, so you get nearly the same reply every time.
High (near 2): more random and creative. Unlikely words get picked more often, so replies vary a lot, and eventually turn into nonsense.

How does temperature actually work?

The model writes its reply one token (a word or piece of a word) at a time. At each step it gives every possible next token a probability. After “The sky is”, that might look like blue 80%, clear 10%, falling 0.1%…

Temperature reshapes those odds before one token is drawn at random:

  • Below 1 sharpens them, so the favorite wins even more often. At 0 the model simply takes the top choice.
  • At 1 the odds stay exactly as the model produced them.
  • Above 1 flattens them, so long shots get a real chance. Near 2 they win often enough that the reply falls apart.

The name comes from physics: heat a system up and unlikely states become more likely.

In tokens (about ¾ of a word each). Stops high temperatures from rambling on.
